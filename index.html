Common questions

Plain answers. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What does Konnex Solutions do?

Konnex is an offshore talent firm. We build offshore teams, place virtual assistants, run recruitment and handle BPO and sales development for companies in the US, UK and beyond. For US state, local and education agencies, we deliver software and IT projects.

Where is your talent based?

Mostly in Pakistan. English is an official language there and the language of business. Our teams work your business hours: UK clients get natural overlap, and US clients get an evening shift that covers their morning.

How much does an offshore hire cost?

It depends on the role, the hours and the seniority. We quote in writing before anything starts, with the person's pay and our fee shown separately, so you can see exactly what you pay for.

How fast can someone start?

It depends on the role. Common roles such as VAs and SDRs usually move faster than senior or technical hires. We give you a realistic timeline on the first call, not a promise we cannot keep.

What if a hire does not work out?

Tell us early. We talk it through with you and the hire, and if the fit is wrong we restart the search. We agree replacement terms with you in writing before the first person starts.

Do I manage the person, or do you?

You direct the daily work, like you would with any team member. We handle hiring, the employment contract, payroll, HR and regular check-ins on performance.

How do you protect my data?

Every hire signs an NDA. We use least-privilege accounts, multi-factor sign-in and managed access to your tools, and we run an offboarding checklist when someone leaves.

Do you work with government agencies?

Yes, through our SLED IT line. We deliver software, web, accessibility, data, QA and IT staff augmentation for US state agencies, counties, cities, school districts and public colleges. Agency data stays in the US. See SLED IT projects.
