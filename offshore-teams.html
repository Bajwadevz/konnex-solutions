Common questions

Plain answers for hiring managers, HR and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

Who are offshore teams for?

Private companies. That includes SaaS and GovTech companies that sell to government. We do not offer offshore staff to public agencies.

Where are the teams based?

In Pakistan, with working hours that overlap US business hours.

How quickly can a team start?

Most teams start in 2 to 4 weeks, depending on roles and headcount.

Who manages the team day to day?

You direct the work. We handle recruiting, payroll, HR and replacements, and check in on performance.

Can offshore staff work on government data?

No. For companies that sell to government, our teams work with public information and your own systems, never with agency data or agency systems.

Planning an offshore team?

Tell us the roles and hours. We will send a team plan and pricing.
