Your team, without the hiring overhead

Hiring locally is slow and expensive. Hiring abroad on your own means contracts, payroll and compliance in a country you do not know.

Konnex sits in between. We recruit vetted people in Pakistan, employ them and handle payroll and HR. Each person works only for you, on your tools, during your hours.

You stay in control of the work. We stay accountable for the people side: fit, performance check-ins and cover when someone is out.

Good fit if you

  • Need 1 to 20 people for repeatable or skilled work
  • Want people dedicated to your company, not shared
  • Work US or UK business hours
  • Want 1 invoice instead of foreign payroll

Common questions

Plain answers. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What is an offshore team?

People in another country who work full time for your company through a partner like Konnex. They are dedicated to you. We employ them and handle payroll and HR.

Why Pakistan?

English-speaking talent, a large IT and services sector and a time zone that suits UK afternoons and US mornings on an evening shift. Pakistan's IT and IT-enabled services exports reached $4.6B in FY2025-26 (State Bank of Pakistan).

Who owns the work my team produces?

You do. Your agreement assigns work product and IP to you, and every team member signs an NDA.

Can I start with 1 person?

Yes. Many clients start with 1 role, then add seats once the setup works.

How is pricing set?

Per person, per month, based on the role and seniority. The quote shows pay and our fee separately.

Can my team work with sensitive data?

Yes, with controls: least-privilege accounts, multi-factor sign-in and managed access to your systems. For regulated data, we confirm the rules with you before anyone starts.
