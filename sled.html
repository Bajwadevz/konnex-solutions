An IT delivery partner for public agencies

Konnex delivers software and IT projects for US state, local and education buyers. We focus on the work agencies can buy quickly: websites and portals, accessibility fixes, small custom apps, data and reporting, testing, and extra engineering capacity.

Large SLED integrators often skip projects under $100,000. Offshore dev shops often ignore public-sector rules. We sit between them: modern delivery at a fair price, with a clear line on what runs offshore and what never does.

Konnex is also an offshore talent firm, so we can scale a project team up or down without long hiring cycles.

How we deliver public-sector IT work

Agency data stays in the US. Projects run in US-hosted cloud environments, and our offshore engineers work only on code and synthetic test data. They never access production systems or agency records. Where a contract or state law requires all work in the US, we tell you up front and staff it that way, or we pass.

Common questions

For IT directors, CIO offices and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What is SLED?

State, local and education government: state agencies, counties, cities, special districts, K-12 districts and public colleges. Read our guide to SLED.

What IT work does Konnex deliver for SLED buyers?

Websites and portals, accessibility remediation, custom applications, data and reporting, QA and testing, IT support and documentation, and IT staff augmentation.

Who works on agency systems and data?

Agency data stays in US-hosted environments. Offshore engineers work only on code and synthetic test data. Where a contract or state law requires all work in the US, we tell you up front and staff it that way, or we pass.

How can our agency buy from you?

By small-purchase quote under your threshold, through an RFP, or with Konnex as a subcontractor to your current prime. See how to buy.

Which NAICS codes do you use?

541511 is our primary code, with 541512, 541519, 518210 and 541611 for IT work, and 561311 and 561320 for staffing. See the capability statement.

Do you work as a subcontractor to prime contractors?

Yes. We supply developers, QA, accessibility and front-end capacity behind primes, under NDA and the prime's flow-down terms.
