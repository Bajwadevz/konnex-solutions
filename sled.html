What we do for public employers

Konnex provides recruitment, contract staffing and executive search to state agencies, counties, cities, special districts, K-12 districts and public colleges across the US. We fill hard roles: IT and cybersecurity, engineering, finance and grants, public works and leadership.

Public hiring has rules a private recruiter often ignores. Minimum qualifications are fixed. Pay bands are published. Positions need approval, and checks must match your policy. We build all of that into intake, so the candidates you see can actually be hired.

Behind each search sits a dedicated sourcing and screening team. It works every business day on your role, which is how we target a shortlist within 5 business days.

US-based. Every placement.

Every professional we place with a public agency lives in the US and is authorized to work here. Work for your agency happens in the US, and your data stays in the US.

How it works

From intake call to first day.

  1. 1Day 1

    Intake

    A 30-minute call with the hiring manager. We confirm duties, minimum qualifications, pay range, location and any civil service or screening rules.

  2. 2Days 2 to 5

    Search and screen

    Our dedicated sourcing and screening team searches, contacts and interviews candidates. Every candidate is US-based and work-authorized.

  3. 3Target: day 5

    Shortlist

    You get 3 to 5 screened candidates with notes on fit, pay expectations and availability. Business days, from a complete intake.

  4. 4Your schedule

    Interview and start

    You interview and choose. We run background checks to your requirements, then handle the offer or the contract start and check in after.

Timelines are targets, not promises. Senior or highly specialized roles take longer. Executive searches follow a staged schedule we agree with you before we start.

Common questions

Plain answers for hiring managers, HR and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What is a SLED staffing agency?

A staffing agency that recruits for state, local and education employers: state agencies, counties, cities, special districts, school districts and public colleges. It works inside public hiring rules such as minimum qualifications, background checks and purchasing thresholds.

What roles can you staff for public agencies?

IT and cybersecurity, engineering and infrastructure, finance and grants, public works and operations, education operations and leadership. See the role lists on each page linked above.

Can you place people permanently, or only on contract?

Both. We run permanent recruitment, contract staffing, contract-to-hire and executive search. Many agencies start on contract while a permanent position is approved.

Is remote work possible for agency roles?

Yes, if your agency allows it. Remote placements are still US-based, work under your accounts and follow your security policies.

How do you protect public data?

We do not need agency data to run a search. Placed staff access your systems only under your accounts and policies. See our delivery and compliance page.

How do we get a quote?

Send the role details. You get a written quote with pricing, timeline and terms, in your format if you have one.
