Leadership searches that reach beyond the job board

The strongest candidates for a public works director or a CIO role are usually employed and not looking. Posting and waiting reaches only the active few.

Konnex runs research-led searches. Our dedicated sourcing team maps sitting leaders in comparable jurisdictions, contacts them directly and screens for fit with your community, your council and your budget.

You get a candidate pool report, a recommended slate and finalist support. You stay in control of every decision.

Typical searches

  • Directors of IT, finance, public works and utilities
  • Assistant city and county managers
  • CIOs and CISOs
  • HR directors
  • District and college administrators

A typical search schedule

An example. We set the real dates with you at kickoff.

  1. 1Weeks 1 to 2

    Kickoff and profile

    Meet with your council, board or hiring authority. Agree the profile, schedule and pay range.

  2. 2Weeks 2 to 6

    Outreach

    Research and direct outreach to sitting leaders, plus advertising where you want it.

  3. 3Weeks 6 to 9

    Screening

    Structured interviews, references and a candidate pool report with a recommended slate.

  4. 4Weeks 9 onward

    Finalists and offer

    Finalist interviews on your schedule, background checks and offer support.

Board and council approval steps can add time. We plan around your meeting calendar.

Common questions

Plain answers for hiring managers, HR and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What roles do you search for?

Department heads and senior leaders: directors of IT, finance, public works, utilities and HR, assistant city and county managers, CIOs and CISOs, and senior district and college administrators.

How long does a search take?

We agree a staged schedule with you before we start, based on your board or council calendar. Senior public searches usually run over several months.

How do you charge?

A fixed fee paid in stages, quoted in your format. No percentage surprises at the end.

Can you respond to our RFP for search services?

Yes. Add us to your bidders list or send the solicitation, and we will respond.

Do you work alongside our HR team or another search firm?

Yes. We can run the full search, or act as the sourcing and research partner to your HR team.
