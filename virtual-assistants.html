Common questions

Plain answers. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What is a managed virtual assistant?

A VA who works for you, while a partner like Konnex handles hiring, contracts, pay and support. You direct the work. We make sure it keeps working.

Can I hire a part-time VA?

Yes. Full-time and part-time setups are both possible. We confirm hours on the first call.

Will my VA work my time zone?

Yes. UK clients get natural overlap. US clients get an evening shift in Pakistan that covers their morning.

How do you vet virtual assistants?

Interviews in English, a written test, a skills task that mirrors your work and reference checks.

What does a VA cost?

It depends on the role and hours. You get a written quote with the VA's pay and our fee shown separately.

Is my data safe with a VA?

Every VA signs an NDA. We set up access with least privilege and multi-factor sign-in, and remove it when they leave.
