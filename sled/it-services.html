Practical IT help, clearly scoped

Agency IT teams carry more systems with fewer people. State CIOs rank modernization, digital services, accessibility and data among their top priorities for 2026 (NASCIO).

Konnex adds capacity where it is safe and useful: environments in US regions, CI/CD pipelines, documentation, testing, reporting and support work your rules allow. We scope each task against your data rules before work starts.

Our CTO, Ali Hassan Mughal, has run 50+ production Linux servers with 24/7 on-call support in a telecom-scale hybrid environment. He holds AWS Solutions Architect Associate and Cisco DevNet Associate certifications.

How we deliver public-sector IT work

Agency data stays in the US. Projects run in US-hosted cloud environments, and our offshore engineers work only on code and synthetic test data. They never access production systems or agency records. Where a contract or state law requires all work in the US, we tell you up front and staff it that way, or we pass.

What we do not take on

We would rather pass than put your agency at risk. Today we do not offer:

  • Production admin access to agency systems from outside the US
  • Work touching criminal justice information (CJIS)
  • Work touching federal tax information (IRS Publication 1075)
  • Cybersecurity operations for agency networks
  • Work under contracts that require all services in the US, unless staffed onshore

For schools and colleges

District and campus IT teams are small and stretched. We help with documentation, website and portal work, reporting, testing and project capacity, so your staff can focus on classrooms and students.

Student records stay under your control. We work from de-identified or synthetic data.

Common questions

For IT directors, CIO offices and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

Do you provide managed IT services for government?

We provide scoped IT services: cloud setup in US regions, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, monitoring, documentation, testing and support capacity. We do not take production admin access from outside the US.

Do you offer DevOps for agencies and districts?

Yes. We set up build and release pipelines, infrastructure as code, monitoring and backup plans, working in your US-region accounts or ones set up for your project.

Can you help K-12 districts?

Yes, with websites, documentation, reporting and project capacity. Student records stay under district control.

Where is agency data stored?

In US-hosted environments, in your accounts or US-region accounts set up for your project.

Do you hold StateRAMP, SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

No. We do not hold those today, and we say so plainly. See our delivery and compliance page for the controls we use.

Can you work with our existing vendor?

Yes. We often add testing, documentation or front-end capacity alongside an existing vendor.
