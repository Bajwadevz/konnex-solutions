Engineering capacity without a long hiring cycle

Public IT teams often have budget for a project but no time to hire for it. Staff augmentation fills the gap for a set term.

Konnex is an offshore talent firm first, so finding and vetting technical people is our core work. We recently placed a remote electrical engineer with a US client in mission-critical infrastructure.

For agency work, we check each role against your contract. Code and test work can run on our hybrid model. Roles that need agency system or data access are staffed in the US where the contract requires it, or we pass.

Common questions

For IT directors, CIO offices and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What is IT staff augmentation?

Adding skilled IT people to your team for a set term. They work on your priorities, while the provider handles hiring and employment.

Can augmented staff access our systems?

Only where your contract and rules allow it. Roles needing agency system or data access are staffed in the US where required. Offshore engineers never access production systems or agency records.

Do you work under state IT staffing programs?

We can work as a subvendor to primes that hold state or cooperative contracts, where those contracts allow subcontractors.

How fast can someone start?

It depends on the role and your onboarding. We give you a realistic timeline once we see the requirements.

How is pricing set?

Hourly or monthly rates per role, agreed in writing before work starts.
