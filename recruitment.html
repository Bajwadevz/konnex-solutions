Common questions

Plain answers. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What is end-to-end recruitment?

We handle every step: the role profile, sourcing, outreach, screening, interviews and offer support. You meet only the candidates worth your time.

What is contract-to-hire?

The person starts on a contract through Konnex. If the fit is right, you convert them to a permanent hire on agreed terms.

Do you run executive search?

Yes, for senior and leadership roles. We map the market, approach people who are not actively looking and present a short slate.

Can you support our in-house recruiters?

Yes. Our sourcers and screeners can work inside your process as extra capacity, including for staffing firms.

How do you charge?

Fees depend on the model and are agreed in writing before work starts. No surprises at the end.
