Our commitments to public agencies

These apply to every agency engagement, without exception.

US-based placements

Every professional we place with a public agency lives in the US and is authorized to work here.

Work performed in the US

Work done for your agency is performed in the US, on site or remotely as your agency allows.

Your data stays in the US

We do not ask for agency data to run a search. Placed staff use your systems under your accounts.

Screening to your rules

Background checks scoped to your policy and the systems the role will touch.

How our recruiting team works

Our recruiting work has 2 sides. The professionals we place work for you, in the US. Our internal recruiting team works for Konnex: it researches public candidate sources, contacts candidates and runs first screens.

Some members of that internal team work remotely from outside the US. They never access agency systems, agency data or work performed for your agency. They handle only candidate information that candidates give us to apply, stored in Konnex systems with access controls and multi-factor sign-in.

If your contract or state rules require all vendor staff on an engagement to be in the US, tell us at intake. We will staff the engagement to match, or tell you plainly that we cannot.

What stays US-only

  • Every placed professional
  • All work performed for your agency
  • All access to agency systems
  • All agency data

Background check process

We match the check to the role and your policy. A typical sequence:

  1. Disclosure and consent. Candidates receive FCRA disclosures and authorize the check in writing.
  2. Identity and work authorization. Form I-9 for contract staff we employ, with E-Verify where your contract requires it.
  3. Criminal history. Searches scoped to your policy, run through a US background screening provider.
  4. Credentials. Education, licenses and certifications the role requires, verified at the source.
  5. References and employment history. Structured reference checks with past supervisors.
  6. Agency checks. We coordinate with your fingerprinting, drug screening or CJIS process. Final approval stays with your agency.

If a check returns something that may affect a decision, we follow the FCRA adverse action process before anything is final.

Rules we plan around

Some public data comes with strict rules on who may access it and from where. We confirm which apply at intake.

What we do not claim

We would rather tell you what we lack than let you find out later.

  • No SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification today
  • No MBE, DBE, HUB or similar certification
  • No cooperative contract awards of our own
  • No agency client logos or testimonials on this site

Common questions

Plain answers for hiring managers, HR and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

Are any of your placed staff outside the US?

No. Every professional we place with a public agency lives in the US and is authorized to work here.

Who on your side sees our data?

Nobody needs your agency data to run a search. Only the professionals you place access your systems, under your accounts and policies.

Do you hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

No. We do not hold those certifications today. This page describes the controls we do use.

Do you hold diversity or small business certifications?

No. We do not claim MBE, DBE, HUB or similar certifications.

Yes. Send it with your quote request and we will review it before work starts.
