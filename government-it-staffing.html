How it works

4 steps, with a 5 business day shortlist target.

  1. 1Day 1

    Intake

    A 30-minute call with the hiring manager. We confirm duties, minimum qualifications, pay range, location and any civil service or screening rules.

  2. 2Days 2 to 5

    Search and screen

    Our dedicated sourcing and screening team searches, contacts and interviews candidates. Every candidate is US-based and work-authorized.

  3. 3Target: day 5

    Shortlist

    You get 3 to 5 screened candidates with notes on fit, pay expectations and availability. Business days, from a complete intake.

  4. 4Your schedule

    Interview and start

    You interview and choose. We run background checks to your requirements, then handle the offer or the contract start and check in after.

Timelines are targets, not promises. Senior or highly specialized roles take longer. Executive searches follow a staged schedule we agree with you before we start.

Common questions

Plain answers for hiring managers, HR and procurement. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

Do you support state IT staff augmentation programs?

Many states route IT staffing through managed service providers or contract vehicles. Where those programs accept subvendors, we can work under them. Ask us about your state.

Can your IT staff work remotely?

If your agency allows it. They remain US-based, use your devices or approved access methods and follow your policies.

Can you meet CJIS screening requirements?

We coordinate with your agency for fingerprint-based checks. Final approval for CJIS access sits with your agency.

How quickly can a contractor start?

For common roles, our target is a shortlist within 5 business days. Start dates then depend on your onboarding and checks.

Do you deliver IT projects?

We place people. We do not take on fixed-scope IT project delivery.
