About Konnex Solutions

An offshore talent firm, founded in 2024. We find the perfect talent match for growing companies, and deliver IT projects for US public agencies.

Book a call

What we do

Konnex finds, hires and manages offshore talent for companies in the US, UK and worldwide. We build offshore teams, place virtual assistants, run recruitment, and handle BPO and sales development.

Our main growth focus is SLED IT projects: software, web, accessibility and IT work for US state, local and education agencies, with agency data kept in the US. We have not yet delivered a SLED IT project, and we will not show one until we have.

Where our people work

Most of our talent is in Pakistan. We say that plainly. English is an official language there, the IT and services sector is large and growing, and the time zone suits UK afternoons and US mornings on an evening shift.

We serve clients in the US and UK.

How we vet talent

Every candidate goes through an interview in English, a written test, a skills task that mirrors the real work and reference checks. We hire for judgment and communication as much as for skills.

How we work

We price in writing. We say what we cannot do. We do not claim clients, credentials or numbers we cannot back up, which is why you will not find logos or testimonials here yet.
