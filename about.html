About Konnex Solutions

A founder-led recruitment firm. We find the perfect talent match for the public agencies that keep communities running.

Request talentHow to buy

What we do

Konnex recruits for US state, local and education employers. We run permanent recruitment, contract staffing and executive search for hard-to-fill roles in IT and cybersecurity, engineering, finance and grants, public works and leadership.

Every professional we place with a public agency is US-based. A dedicated sourcing and screening team works each search every business day, so shortlists arrive faster and cost less to produce.

We also run a separate line that builds offshore teams for growing companies. It serves private companies only.

Our approach

Public hiring has rules. We learn yours at intake, then present candidates who meet them. We price in writing, we say what we cannot do, and we do not claim credentials we do not hold.
